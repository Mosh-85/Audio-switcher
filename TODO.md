# Future ideas

## Wireless devices and groups

- [ ] Preserve saved device choices when editing a group while a device is disconnected.
- [ ] Keep a selected group pending when its wireless device is unavailable,
  then apply the missing device automatically when it reconnects.
  - Keep the current default for unavailable devices and show a waiting status.
  - Cancel the pending switch when another group is selected.
  - Decide how manual device changes in KDE should affect a pending switch.
  - Consider how the app, tray and widget should show pending and active states.
